# Nets

## Monday Nights at 19:30

### SARES Net

SARES meets every Monday at 19:30 (Sunnyvale, CA, USA). Guests and prospective menbers are welcome.

During <span id="this_month"></span>, we will meet on one of these frequencies:

| Frequency                        | Offset   | CTCSS   |                            SARES Channel                            |
| -------------------------------- | -------- | ------- | :-----------------------------------------------------------------: |
| 145.170 MHz                      | Negative | 94.8 Hz |                                  1                                  |
| 444.525 MHz                      | Positive | 94.8 Hz |                                 12                                  |
| <span id="frequency"></span> MHz | Simplex  |         | <span id="channel_number"></span> (<span id="channel_name"></span>) |

Please see the [SARES Frequency List](https://github.com/saresrg/Go-Kit-Forms/releases/latest) for full details.

### SPECS Net

At 20:00, we transition to the [SPECS Monday Night Net](https://www.specsnet.org/monday-night-net).

After that, typically between 20:10 and 20:20, we return to SARES Channel 1 to gather late check-ins and conduct any remaining business.

## Tuesday Nights at 20:00

### SVECS Net

The Silicon Valley Emergency Communications System Net ([SVECS Net](https://www.svecs.net/)) meets every Tuesday at 20:00.

The SARES weekly check-in count from the preceding Monday night will be carried over and reported by a SARES representative. If you missed the weekly Monday net and want to check in on Tuesday, you need to do so as "late checkins for Sunnyvale" which will be called on the main SVECS net right after the Sunnyvale representative has reported in.
